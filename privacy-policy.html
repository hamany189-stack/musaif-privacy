Privacy Policy - Musif (Musaif)

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Musif ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, collects, and processes, how it is used, and our strict data retention practices.

Data We Access & Collect

  • Health Data & Health Connect: Musif accesses and reads vital physiological data, specifically heart rate measurements, using the android.permission.health.READ_HEART_RATE permission via Health Connect by Android. This permission is requested only with your explicit consent.
  • Photos: Images you specifically capture or select from your device to assess injuries and wounds during emergency triage.
  • Voice Conversations: The microphone is utilized solely to interact with the emergency voice assistant when activated by you.
  • CPR Training Camera Data: The camera is used to analyze chest compression posture and rate during CPR training mode, processed on-device where technically possible.
  • Location Data: Used strictly when necessary to help locate nearby emergency medical services or facilitate emergency dispatch.

How We Use Health Data & Information

All accessed data is used strictly for immediate first-aid and emergency assistance features:

  • Real-Time Vital Signs Monitoring: Heart rate data read via Health Connect is displayed live on the emergency triage screen to monitor the injured person's physical stability and guide CPR protocols.
  • Emergency AI Guidance: Photos and voice inputs are processed to provide real-time first-aid guidance and triage recommendations.
  • No Secondary Use: Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, tracking, user profiling, or credit evaluation.

Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Musif does not store or retain user personal or health data on any external servers (We do not store or retain user data).

  • All heart rate measurements, vital readings, and injury images are processed ephemerally and locally on your device during the active first-aid session.
  • Once the emergency session is concluded or the application is closed, the in-session health readings are discarded.
  • You have complete control over Health Connect data and can view, manage, or revoke access permissions at any time through your Android system settings (Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Health Connect).

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, license, or share your personal or health data with third parties. Any processing required for real-time analysis is transmitted through industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and is treated with strict confidentiality.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical safeguards to protect any transient data processed by the application against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Your Rights

Because we do not persistently store personal or health data on external databases, there is no centralized personal record maintained. If you have any inquiries regarding data handling or privacy practices, you may reach out to us directly.
